# Privacy Policy — VibeCafe

**The published policy lives at <https://vibecafe.vercel.app/privacy>.**

That page is the authoritative version — the one users are shown, and the one that
governs. It is served from [`public/privacy.html`](./public/privacy.html); edit that
file to change the policy. This file is a pointer, deliberately kept short so there is
never a second copy of the text to fall out of date.

## The short version

- No account, no sign-in, no server.
- No analytics, no tracking, no cookies. Nothing measures you.
- Custom scenes, playlists and presets are saved in your browser's `localStorage` and
  never leave your device. Clearing site data deletes all of it.
- Your browser fetches files from Vercel, jsDelivr, Mixkit, raw.githack.com and Google
  Fonts, which see your IP address as they would on any site.
- Playlist embeds you add yourself load Spotify / Apple Music / YouTube / SoundCloud,
  which then collect data under their own policies.
