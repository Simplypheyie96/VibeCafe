# Privacy Policy — VibeCafe

**Last updated: 26 July 2026**

> ⚠️ **This is a starter draft, not legal advice.** It was written to describe what
> the app actually does today, and it has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Before you
> rely on it for anything serious — a public launch, an app-store listing, ads,
> payments, or any users in the EU/UK/California — have a qualified professional
> review it. Some jurisdictions require specific disclosures this draft does not make.

VibeCafe is a browser-based lofi music and ambient-sound player. This policy explains
what it collects, what it doesn't, and what you can do about it.

---

## The short version

- There is no account, no login, and no password.
- Your settings stay in your own browser. They are never uploaded to us.
- We use Google Analytics to count usage in aggregate. You can block it.
- We do not sell your data, because we do not have data to sell.

---

## What VibeCafe stores on your device

VibeCafe uses your browser's `localStorage`. This data stays on your device, is
readable only by this site, and is never transmitted to a VibeCafe server — there is
no VibeCafe server. It holds:

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Custom scenes you create | So your scenes survive a page reload |
| Playlists you add | So your embedded playlists survive a reload |
| Saved presets | So your saved ambient mixes survive a reload |
| Volume levels | So the app doesn't reset your mix each visit |
| Install-prompt dismissal | So we don't re-ask after you've said no |

**To erase all of it:** clear site data for this domain in your browser settings.
That returns the app to a completely fresh state. Nothing is retained elsewhere.

---

## Analytics

VibeCafe uses **Google Analytics 4** to understand how the app is used in aggregate —
how many people visit, which scenes are popular, whether features get used at all.

Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your IP address, which Google uses to derive
an approximate location. It does **not** receive your name, email address, or anything
you type into VibeCafe. We use it to look at totals, not individuals.

Events sent include: page views, scene changes, play/pause, ambient toggles, and
playlist actions. Each carries only the name of the scene, track, or control involved.

Google's own handling of this data is governed by their policies:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy>

**To opt out:** use any tracking blocker, your browser's "do not track" or
anti-tracking settings, or Google's official opt-out add-on at
<https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout>. VibeCafe works normally with analytics
blocked — nothing depends on it.

## Third parties your browser contacts

Using VibeCafe causes your browser to make requests to these services. Each one sees
your IP address, as any web request does. We do not send them anything else about you.

| Service | What for | Their policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Vercel** | Hosting the app itself | <https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy> |
| **Internet Archive** | Streaming the Creative Commons music | <https://archive.org/about/terms> |
| **Google Fonts** | The Space Grotesk typeface | <https://policies.google.com/privacy> |
| **Google Analytics** | Aggregate usage stats | <https://policies.google.com/privacy> |
| **Mixkit / jsDelivr** | Ambient sound files | <https://mixkit.co/terms> · <https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net> |

If you add a Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, or SoundCloud playlist, that service's
embedded player loads inside VibeCafe and is governed by **their** privacy policy, not
this one. Those embeds can set their own cookies. Removing the playlist stops the embed
from loading.

---

## What VibeCafe does not do

- No account creation, and no email address is ever requested.
- No advertising, and no ad networks.
- No selling, renting, or sharing of personal data.
- No microphone, camera, or location access.
- No server-side profile — there is no server.

---

## Children

VibeCafe is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal
information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through
this app, contact us and we will look into it.

---

## Your rights

Because VibeCafe holds no personal data about you on any server, most data-subject
requests (access, deletion, portability) are satisfied by clearing your browser's site
data — you already hold the only copy. For the analytics data Google holds, use Google's
own controls linked above.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, or the
CCPA/CPRA. **This draft does not implement the consent banners, opt-out links, or
disclosures those laws may require.** That is one of the main reasons to get it reviewed
before a serious launch.
