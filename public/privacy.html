Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 July 2026

VibeCafe has no accounts, no sign-in, no tracking and no analytics. There is no server that stores anything about you, because there is no server — the whole app runs in your browser.

Who is responsible for this app

VibeCafe is made and published by Ajayi Feyikemi Mabel, an individual developer, as a free personal project. For anything in this policy — including a request to access or delete data — write to ajayifey@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There is no account and nothing to sign up for.
  • Nothing you make in VibeCafe is uploaded anywhere. Custom scenes, playlists and presets are saved in your browser's own storage and never leave your device.
  • There is no analytics and no tracking of any kind — no Google Analytics, no pixels, no advertising, no fingerprinting.
  • VibeCafe sets no cookies.
  • Your browser does fetch files — music, images, fonts — from the services listed further down, which see your IP address the way they would on any website.

What VibeCafe stores on your device

These are saved with your browser's localStorage, under the keys shown. They stay on the device you used, are not synced between your devices, and are never transmitted.

WhatStorage keyWhy
Custom scenes you addvibecafe_custom_scenesSo your own scenes are still there next time
Playlist links you addvibecafe_custom_playlistsSo your embedded playlists persist
Saved presetsvibecafe_presetsSo a saved mix of scene, sounds and levels can be recalled
Whether you dismissed the install promptvibecafe_install_dismissedSo the prompt does not keep reappearing after you say no

VibeCafe also registers a service worker, which keeps a copy of the app's own files in your browser's cache so it loads quickly and works offline. That cache holds application files only — no personal information.

To erase all of it: clear site data for this site in your browser settings, or use your browser's "Clear browsing data" for cookies and site data. Everything above disappears immediately and permanently. Since none of it was ever sent anywhere, that is the whole deletion.

Analytics: there are none

VibeCafe does not measure you. No analytics service is loaded, no measurement or tracking script runs, no events are recorded, and no usage data is collected or shared. Nobody — including the author — can see which scenes you play, how long you stay, or that you visited at all.

There is a dormant, unconfigured analytics integration in the open-source code. It is switched off and ships with no measurement ID, so nothing is loaded and nothing is sent. If that ever changes, this page will say so before it goes live, and the "last updated" date will change.

Services your browser contacts

VibeCafe is a static site with no backend. To play music and show artwork, your browser requests files directly from the services below. Each one necessarily sees your IP address, your browser's user-agent, and which file you asked for — the same basic information any website receives. None of them receive your name, an account, or anything you created in the app.

If you embed your own playlist

VibeCafe lets you paste in a Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube or SoundCloud playlist. Doing that loads that service's own embedded player inside the page, and from then on you are using that service: it can set its own cookies, may recognise you if you are signed in to it, and collects data under its own privacy policy, not this one. YouTube embeds use youtube-nocookie.com, its reduced-tracking domain.

This only happens if you add an embed yourself. A visitor who never touches the playlist feature never loads any of it.

What VibeCafe never does

  • Ask for your name, email address, or any other personal detail.
  • Create an account or a profile of you.
  • Set advertising, marketing or tracking cookies.
  • Sell or share personal information. There is none to sell.
  • Use your microphone, camera, location, contacts, or files.
  • Upload the images or links you add.

Children

VibeCafe is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age. Because it collects nothing and has no accounts, there is nothing about a child for us to hold or delete.

Your rights

Data-protection laws — including Nigeria's Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, the EU and UK GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act — give you rights to access, correct, delete, and port your personal data, and to object to its processing.

Those rights attach to personal data held by an organisation. VibeCafe holds none: it has no server, no database, no logs of you and no account records, so there is no file to send you and nothing to correct. Everything the app saves is on your own device, and clearing your site data removes it in full and at once, with no request needed.

If you think that is wrong — if you believe VibeCafe holds something about you — write to ajayifey@gmail.com and it will be looked into. You can also complain to your data-protection authority; in Nigeria that is the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Changes to this policy

If VibeCafe's data practices change, this page changes with them and the date at the top is updated. A change that introduces any collection of personal data will be described here before it takes effect, not after.
