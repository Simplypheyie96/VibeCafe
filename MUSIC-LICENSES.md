# Music & Sound Licensing — VibeCafe

**Last updated: 26 July 2026**

> ⚠️ **Starter draft, not legal advice.** The licence assignments below come from the
> metadata each rights holder published with their release, and every link was checked
> to resolve. That is a good-faith record, not a legal opinion. If VibeCafe is ever
> used commercially, have a qualified professional review this — and re-verify the
> licences directly with the rights holders.

---

## Where the music comes from

Every built-in track is a **permissively licensed release from the Internet Archive's
`netlabels` collection** — netlabels are record labels that publish under Creative
Commons by design. Tracks were selected by searching for permissive licence URLs only,
then filtering by mood tags and checking that each file streams with CORS enabled.

**206 tracks across 9 moods, one mood per scene.**

### Why not the obvious alternatives

- **YouTube 24/7 lofi radios** — deliberately excluded. Streaming or re-embedding those
  requires a licence VibeCafe does not have, and pulling audio out of YouTube breaks
  their Terms of Service. This was a hard constraint on the project.
- **Spotify / Apple Music** — their embedded players are supported as a *user-added*
  feature, using each service's official embed. No audio is downloaded, cached, or
  re-transmitted. VibeCafe ships with no default content from either.
- **Commercial lofi libraries** — all cost money. The requirement was zero budget.

### Licence breakdown

- **CC BY-SA 3.0** — 85 tracks
- **CC BY 3.0** — 61 tracks
- **CC BY-SA 4.0** — 25 tracks
- **CC0 1.0** — 16 tracks
- **CC BY 4.0** — 15 tracks
- **Public Domain Mark** — 4 tracks

### What each licence requires of VibeCafe

| Licence | Attribution required? | What VibeCafe does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CC BY 3.0 / 4.0 | Yes — credit the creator and link the licence | Now-playing card shows the artist, the licence, and links to the source page |
| CC BY-SA 3.0 / 4.0 | Yes, plus ShareAlike on *derivatives* | Same. VibeCafe streams tracks unmodified, so it creates no derivative and ShareAlike is not triggered |
| CC0 1.0 | No | Credited anyway |
| Public Domain Mark | No | Credited anyway |

Attribution appears **in the app, while each track plays** — artist name, licence name
linked to the licence deed, and a link back to the source release page. That satisfies
CC BY 4.0 §3(a)(1) and the equivalent clause in 3.0.

### If you want to reuse a track

Listening through VibeCafe grants you nothing extra. Go to the track's source page,
read its licence, and comply with it directly.
